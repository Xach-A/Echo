Echo - free virtual audio mixer and voice changer

What this installer does
  * Installs the Echo application for your user account (no administrator rights needed).
  * Adds Start menu shortcuts and an uninstaller.

What it does NOT do
  * It installs no drivers, services or background updaters.
  * It does not change your Windows default playback or recording devices.
  * It downloads nothing. Echo itself goes online only when you click one of its
    download buttons (Install VB-CABLE, Install the AI engine, Download ContentVec)
    or Check for updates, and checks every download before keeping it. No accounts,
    ads or telemetry.

Virtual microphone (Discord, Roblox, OBS, games)
  Other apps hear Echo through a virtual audio cable driver, a separate product.
  Echo's setup guide has an Install VB-CABLE button: it downloads VB-Audio's free
  VB-CABLE and opens VB-Audio's own installer, which asks Windows for administrator
  approval. See docs\VIRTUAL_DEVICES.md.

AI voices (optional)
  Need Microsoft's ONNX Runtime (Install the AI engine, in Voices > AI voice) and a
  voice model you are allowed to use. Neither is bundled. See docs\AI_VOICE.md.
