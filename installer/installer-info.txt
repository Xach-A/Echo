Echo - free virtual audio mixer and voice changer

What this installer does
  * Installs the Echo application for your user account (no administrator rights needed).
  * Adds Start menu shortcuts and an uninstaller.

What it does NOT do
  * It installs no drivers, services or background updaters.
  * It does not change your Windows default playback or recording devices.
  * It does not download anything and Echo makes no network connections.

Virtual microphone / virtual speaker
  Other apps (Discord, OBS, games) can use Echo's output as a microphone through a
  virtual audio cable driver. That driver is a separate product (for example the free
  VB-CABLE from vb-audio.com) that you install yourself; it needs administrator
  approval. Echo detects it automatically. See docs\VIRTUAL_DEVICES.md.

AI voice conversion (optional)
  Needs ONNX Runtime and a voice model you are licensed to use. Neither is bundled.
  See docs\AI_VOICE.md.
