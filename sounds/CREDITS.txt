Echo's built-in soundboard sounds
=================================

These are real recordings and game sounds by the people below, not made by
Echo and not AI-generated. The BigSoundBank sounds are field and studio
recordings (each sound's page lists the recorder and microphone used); the
others were published on OpenGameArt, Kenney and Wikimedia Commons in
2016-2020, before AI sound generators were available, except the ta-da, which
its author made in GarageBand with its trumpet sounds (2025).

All but one are dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); sad_trombone.wav is
licensed under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Echo trimmed each sound to length (with a fade-out where it was cut), removed
DC offset, set the level and converted it to 48 kHz 16-bit WAV
(tools/prepare_sounds.py). buzzer.wav was also saturated and low-passed, which
turns the recorded hum into a game-show buzz, and tada.wav is the last two
notes of its fanfare. Nothing else was changed.
